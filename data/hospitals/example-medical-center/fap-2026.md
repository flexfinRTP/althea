# Example Medical Center 2026 Financial Assistance Policy

**Demonstration Policy.** This document is fictional. It is modeled on nonprofit-hospital FAP structures for the Althea ETHOnline demo. It is not a real hospital policy.

## Who May Apply

Insured and uninsured patients may apply for financial assistance for eligible emergency and medically necessary care.

## Free Care

Households at or below 150% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines may receive free care for eligible services.

## Discounted Care

Households from 151% through 400% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines may receive discounted care on a sliding scale.

For the published demonstration example (charges $18,420, household of 3, annual income $51,000), discounted care yields $15,950 in assistance and a $2,470 remainder.

## How to Apply

Complete the hospital's Financial Assistance Application. Attach proof of income, household size, and the billing statement. Submit by mail, in person, or online.
