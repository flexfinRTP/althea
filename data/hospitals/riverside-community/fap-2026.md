# Riverside Community Hospital 2026 Financial Assistance Policy

**Demonstration Policy.** This document is fictional. It exists to show that Althea's eligibility engine is not hardcoded to the Example Medical Center demo case. It is not a real hospital policy.

## Who May Apply

Uninsured patients may apply for financial assistance for eligible emergency and medically necessary care. Insured patients are not eligible under this demonstration policy.

## Free Care

Uninsured households at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines may receive free care for eligible services.

## Discounted Care

Uninsured households from 201% through 300% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines may receive a 60% discount.

## How to Apply

Complete the hospital's Financial Assistance Application. Attach proof of income. Submit by mail or in person.
